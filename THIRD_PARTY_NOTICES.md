# Third-party assets

The application self-hosts the following font files; no font CDN is contacted:

- Inter, distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. License: `frontend/assets/fonts/inter-LICENSE.txt`.
- JetBrains Mono, distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. License: `frontend/assets/fonts/jetbrains-mono-LICENSE.txt`.

Files were obtained from their corresponding `@fontsource` packages. The application and test dependencies retain their respective upstream licenses. Dependency versions are recorded in the requirements and package lock files.
